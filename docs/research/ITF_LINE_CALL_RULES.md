# ITF Official Rules of Tennis (2026 Edition) — Line Calling & Court Geometry Reference

## 1. Authoritative Citations & Overview
This reference specifies the court dimensions, line widths, ball specifications, and officiating rules according to the official **International Tennis Federation (ITF) Rules of Tennis (2026)**.

---

## 2. ITF Rule 1: The Court

### 2.1 Court Dimensions
- **Length**: $23.77\text{ m}$ ($78.0\text{ ft}$)
- **Singles Width**: $8.23\text{ m}$ ($27.0\text{ ft}$)
- **Doubles Width**: $10.97\text{ m}$ ($36.0\text{ ft}$)
- **Net Height**: $0.914\text{ m}$ ($3.0\text{ ft}$) at the center; $1.07\text{ m}$ ($3.5\text{ ft}$) at net posts ($0.914\text{ m}$ outside doubles court).
- **Service Line Distance**: $6.40\text{ m}$ ($21.0\text{ ft}$) from net on each side ($Y = 5.485\text{ m}$ and $Y = 18.285\text{ m}$).
- **Service Box Width**: $4.115\text{ m}$ ($13.5\text{ ft}$) on each side of the Center Service Line.

### 2.2 Line Widths & Placement Rules
- **General Line Width**: Between $2.5\text{ cm}$ ($1\text{ in}$) and $5.0\text{ cm}$ ($2\text{ in}$).
- **Baselines**: Up to $10.0\text{ cm}$ ($4\text{ in}$) wide.
- **Center Service Line**: Must be exactly $5.0\text{ cm}$ ($2\text{ in}$) wide.
- **Center Mark**: $10.0\text{ cm}$ ($4\text{ in}$) long and $5.0\text{ cm}$ ($2\text{ in}$) wide, drawn inside baseline.
- **Measurement Convention (CRITICAL)**: **"All court measurements shall be made to the outside of the lines."**
  - This means lines are drawn **inside** the outer court perimeter boundaries.
  - The outer edge of the sideline is at $X = 1.37\text{ m}$ and $X = 9.60\text{ m}$.
  - The outer edge of the baseline is at $Y = 0.00\text{ m}$ and $Y = 23.77\text{ m}$.
  - The outer edge of the service line (away from net) is at $Y = 5.485\text{ m}$ and $Y = 18.285\text{ m}$.

---

## 3. ITF Rule 12: Ball Touches a Line
- **Text**: *"If a ball touches a line, it is regarded as touching the court bounded by that line."*
- **Legal Implication**: If any physical portion of the dynamic ball-court contact patch touches any portion of the painted boundary line (from inner edge to outer edge), the ball is legally **`IN`**.

---

## 4. ITF Appendix I: The Ball Specifications
- **Mass**: $56.0 - 59.4\text{ grams}$
- **Size / Diameter**: $6.54 - 6.86\text{ cm}$ ($2.57 - 2.70\text{ in}$)
  - Nominal Diameter: $6.70\text{ cm}$ ($0.0670\text{ m}$)
  - Nominal Ball Radius ($R_{ball}$): $3.35\text{ cm}$ ($0.0335\text{ m}$)
- **Rebound Bounce**: $135 - 147\text{ cm}$ when dropped from $254\text{ cm}$ onto concrete ($e \approx 0.73 - 0.76$).
- **Forward Deformation**: $0.56 - 0.74\text{ cm}$ under $8.165\text{ kg}$ static load.
- **Dynamic Contact Radius**: Under high-velocity oblique impact ($15 - 30\text{ m/s}$), the dynamic contact footprint radius is $r_c \approx 1.0 - 2.0\text{ cm}$, significantly smaller than the full ball radius $R_{ball} = 3.35\text{ cm}$.
