## New concrete lead: Tracking by Deblatting

The [author project](https://cmp.felk.cvut.cz/fmo/) links separate TbD, TbD-3D and Falling Objects releases, including high-speed/low-speed images and ground truth. The [author-deposited IJCV paper](https://www.research-collection.ethz.ch/server/api/core/bitstreams/6cbfa6be-abc9-4ed5-9d9d-4f112c88fe10/content), sections 5 and 8.2, describes 240 FPS recording and 30 FPS images obtained by averaging eight exposures. Its sequence table includes tennis, throw-tennis and hit-tennis examples. These are recorded temporal-image references, rather than a synthetic rendering dataset. The paper's separate tennis-radar experiment joins spectator footage to a different video showing speeds, uses manual court scale and assumes 29.97 FPS. It does not establish original PTS, synchronization or reusable footage rights for this project. No paper score is a project result.

The [benchmark repository](https://github.com/rozumden/fmo-deblurring-benchmark) is now pinned to commit `6c9f277e15ff191f34067ad82811f94dabe16baf`. Its software [license](https://github.com/rozumden/fmo-deblurring-benchmark/blob/6c9f277e15ff191f34067ad82811f94dabe16baf/LICENSE) is MIT; that grant names software and associated documentation. A separate rights grant covering the linked external footage/annotations was not established. The archive downloader was read, retained and never run.

Actual pinned loader-text inspection establishes a **2D image-trajectory interface**: `gt.txt`, optional radius/template or bbox-derived radii, high-speed PNG indexing, and grouping of eight or twelve subframes. It does not establish released calibrated metric 3D coordinates, a camera-to-world scale, a synchronized radar table, clock uncertainty or independent per-trial physical speed. The author evaluation also uses target-derived bounding regions/object size and can reverse temporal direction against the target. Its benchmark procedure therefore cannot be adopted as blind end-to-end speed qualification. Sources: [loader schema](https://github.com/rozumden/fmo-deblurring-benchmark/blob/6c9f277e15ff191f34067ad82811f94dabe16baf/benchmark/reporters.py), [evaluation consumer](https://github.com/rozumden/fmo-deblurring-benchmark/blob/6c9f277e15ff191f34067ad82811f94dabe16baf/benchmark/benchmark_loader.py), [helpers](https://github.com/rozumden/fmo-deblurring-benchmark/blob/6c9f277e15ff191f34067ad82811f94dabe16baf/benchmark/loaders_helpers.py).

Decision: retain this as a high-speed image-trajectory lead. Do not acquire media/targets for final physical-speed evaluation until external-data rights, original capture timing and independent metric calibration are established. It is not evidence for the supported full singles-match view. No train/test assignment was created.
