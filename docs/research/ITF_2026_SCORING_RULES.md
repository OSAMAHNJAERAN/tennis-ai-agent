# ITF Official Rules of Tennis (2026 Edition) — Scoring & Match State Reference

## 1. Official Authority & Citations
This document summarizes the authoritative match scoring, service order, tie-break, and point resolution rules defined in the **ITF Rules of Tennis (2026)**.

---

## 2. Rule 5: Score in a Game

### 2.1 Standard Game
In a standard game, the score is called with the server's score first:
- **No points won**: "Love" (0)
- **First point won**: "15" (1)
- **Second point won**: "30" (2)
- **Third point won**: "40" (3)
- **Fourth point won**: "Game" (4, provided a 2-point margin exists)

#### Deuce & Advantage:
- If both players win 3 points ($3-3$), the score is **"Deuce"**.
- The player winning the next point holds **"Advantage"** ("Advantage Server" or "Advantage Receiver").
- If that player wins the subsequent point, they win the **Game**.
- If the opposing player wins the point, the score reverts to **Deuce**.
- A player must win two consecutive points from Deuce to win the game.

### 2.2 Tie-Break Game (7-Point Standard)
- During a tie-break game, points are scored numerically: $0, 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7, 8...$
- The first player to reach **7 points** with a **margin of at least 2 points** wins the tie-break game and the set (e.g. $7-5, 8-6, 12-10$).
- If the score reaches $6-6$, play continues until one player achieves a 2-point lead.

## 3. Rule 6: Score in a Set

### 3.1 Tie-Break Set (Standard Production Format)
- The first player to win **6 games** wins that set, provided there is a margin of at least two games (e.g., $6-0, 6-1, 6-2, 6-3, 6-4$).
- If the score reaches $5-5$, a player winning the next two games wins the set $7-5$.
- If the score reaches $6-6$, a **Tie-Break Game** is played to determine the set winner ($7-6$).

### 3.2 Advantage Set (Alternative Format)
- The first player to win 6 games with a 2-game margin wins the set.
- If $5-5$ or $6-6$ is reached, play continues without a tie-break until one player achieves a 2-game lead (e.g. $8-6, 11-9$).

---

## 4. Rule 7: Score in a Match
- **Best-of-3 Sets**: The match is won by the player who first wins **2 sets**.
- **Best-of-5 Sets**: The match is won by the player who first wins **3 sets**.
- **Match Tie-Break (10-Point)**: An approved alternative where a deciding final set is replaced by a 10-point tie-break (first to 10 points with 2-point lead).

## 5. Rules 8, 14, 16, 17, 20, 21: Service Mechanics & Rotation

### 5.1 Standard Game Service Rotation (Rule 14)
- Throughout each standard game, the **same player serves**.
- At the end of each game, the receiver becomes the server and the server becomes the receiver for the next game.

### 5.2 Service Court Alternation (Rule 16 & 17)
- The server starts each standard game by serving from the **right (Deuce)** side of their baseline diagonally into the receiver's Deuce service box.
- The service side alternates every point:
  - **Even total points played in game** ($0, 2, 4, 6...$): **Deuce (Right) side**.
  - **Odd total points played in game** ($1, 3, 5, 7...$): **Ad (Left) side**.

### 5.3 First vs Second Service (Rules 19 & 20)
- **First Service Fault**: If the first service lands OUT or in the net, it is a **First Fault**. **NO POINT IS AWARDED**. The server receives a **Second Service** from the same side of the court.
- **Double Fault**: If the second service is also a fault, the server commits a **Double Fault**, and the **Receiver wins the point**.

### 5.4 Service Let (Rule 22)
- If a service touches the net tape and lands in the correct service box, it is a **Service Let**.
- The service attempt does not count: the server replays the same service attempt ($1^{\text{st}}$ or $2^{\text{nd}}$) with no point awarded.

### 5.5 Tie-Break Service Rotation (Rule 5.b & Rule 14)
- **Point 1**: Served by the player due to serve, from the **Right (Deuce)** court.
- **Points 2 & 3**: Served by the opposing player (Point 2 from **Ad**, Point 3 from **Deuce**).
- **Subsequent Points**: Players alternate serving **two consecutive points** each (first from Ad, then from Deuce) until the tie-break concludes.

## 6. Rule 24: Player Loses Point
A player loses the point if:
1. The server serves two consecutive faults (Double Fault).
2. The player fails to return the ball in play before it bounces twice consecutively.
3. The player hits the ball such that it hits the ground outside the correct court boundaries.
4. The player returns the ball such that it hits a permanent fixture before bouncing in court.
5. The player touches the net, post, or opponent court while the ball is in play.
