# Broadcast ball development data and bounded learning checks

The accepted source remains the [author's RacketVision dataset](https://huggingface.co/datasets/linfeng302/RacketVision), pinned to revision `85157ca21faa2abca96d837dd2b963738029bcc8`. Its MIT dataset card explicitly describes downloading videos and ball CSV labels for training. This collection is used for private local academic development. Original broadcast redistribution rights are not separately verified; raw video and derived media are not public examples. No duplicate video downloads were needed.

The 60 previously acquired publisher training clips were checksum-checked and reviewed for camera scope. The eight selected singles clips represent six visible event/court contexts. Doubles observed in `match174` and `match187` were excluded. Native selected frames were checked for elevated baseline view and retained at 1920×1080, with original FFprobe PTS and time bases. The raw publisher CSV uses zero-indexed frame numbers and visible-ball centers, rather than box labels.

The visible event/pair is a conservative grouping aid. Dates, seasons, rounds, complete original-match identities and independence from other historic project data are not fully verified. All Barcelona pairings share a conservative session group. St Petersburg stays development only. The set has **71 training images and 10 development images**, each with one visible box, and no final-test frames. These groups are not a frozen 60/20/20 qualification split.

Publisher centers were the label starting point; **Codex manually selected the boxes** after inspecting 8× nearest-neighbor native crops. Fifteen of 96 selected candidates were excluded because of ambiguous lines, net, player overlap or background evidence. The 81 accepted boxes are agent visual annotations, not independently reviewed human box ground truth. No model predictions, interpolated centers or imagined hidden-ball observations were accepted. There are no independently reviewed negative images in this pilot; absent/occluded frames are not silently converted into negatives.

One alternative development experiment uses the same YOLO26m with native640×640 crops. It preserves native ball pixels and varies target placement using seeded jitter, with per-frame crop origins and inverse mappings to the original image. Its measured ball median is8×8 native pixels, minimum3×3 and maximum18×21; target centers vary across the image. All81 crops were checked for exact native pixel equality, inverse box mapping, PTS preservation and split integrity in `outputs/yolo26_native_crop_transform_audit_v1.json`. The source manifest is `data/yolo26/pilot_broadcast_native_crop_v1/source_manifest.json`.

These crops use ground-truth target positions. Their metrics are **oracle crop learning diagnostics**, not full-frame ball discovery, a working dynamic crop tracker, complete trajectory coverage, speed evidence or independent qualification. The crop resource guard requires at least1GiB available RAM after CUDA initialization; full-frame training retains its stricter guard. A run remains bounded to four epochs and cannot promote checkpoints. Final-test reservations, including the separately reserved NKUST reference, are not opened by these scripts.

These are the same ten positive frames from one indoor match, not a negative-video benchmark.

The full-frame predictions came within 3.26, 0.64 and 0.63 pixels of the publisher center on frames281,483 and530 respectively, but their maximum box IoUs were only 0.392,0.411 and0.342; none passed the declared match criterion. Other full-frame nearest candidates were 143.51–655.21 pixels away. Small far-end examples at frames56 and163 had native boxes 8×6 and7×7 pixels and were missed on both input types. This result does not justify checkpoint promotion or a larger epoch schedule. Receipt and complete per-frame errors: `outputs/yolo26_ball_native_crop_diagnostic_v1/diagnostic.json`; the two private `review_00.jpg` and `review_01.jpg` sheets were inspected after generation.

A label-independent native-tile discovery diagnostic was then frozen for the same checkpoint and ten development frames, using confidence0.01 and one-to-one IoU0.50. Its eight640×640 regions use horizontal origins0,512,1024,1280 and vertical origins0,440, covering the full1920×1080 image with edge anchoring. Every tile is sent at `imgsz=640`, preserving native scale; there is no target-selected crop or extra full-frame pass. The frozen duplicate rule is IoU≥0.50 **or** center distance≤2 native pixels in stable descending confidence order. The script retains every returned proposal, its tile origin and raw/global boxes, duplicate associations, target IoU and publisher-center errors before and after deduplication, and compares the saved full-frame receipt without another baseline run. Protocol: `outputs/yolo26_ball_native_tile_diagnostic_v1/protocol.json`; script: `scripts/data/diagnose_yolo26_native_tiles.py`.

Additional primary-source data review on9October:

- [Dryad tennis box annotations, DOI10.5061/dryad.3bk3j9m13](https://datadryad.org/dataset/doi:10.5061/dryad.3bk3j9m13): authors provide normalized box labels and state that participants consented to de-identified release. Labels are CC0; linked de-identified videos use CC BY-NC-ND4.0. The tennis annotation download returned403 and [linked Zenodo media](https://zenodo.org/records/19874312) was inaccessible through the web tool. This remains an acquisition candidate for private ball research, with view, overlap and exact media grant to check; it is not a player-pose training corpus because people are blurred.
- [UVY, Zenodo21303900](https://zenodo.org/records/21303900): authors state that20 sports videos were collected under CC BY and labels were manually corrected after model proposals. Tennis viewpoint, classes and overlap with already-inspected MUVY must be checked before assigning any independent split. No UVY media or frame was downloaded or inspected.
- [F³Set author's repository](https://github.com/F3Set/F3Set): useful event timing and player/score metadata, but the checked repository exposes no license and is not a published ball-box corpus. No media was acquired or used for training.
- [Silent Impact author's data](https://github.com/jyp0802/Silent-Impact): explicit CC BY4.0, but the released examples are arm IMU arrays, outside the selected vision-data need; reference only.
