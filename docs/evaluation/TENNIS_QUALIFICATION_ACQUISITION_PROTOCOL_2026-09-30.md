### Recovery requirements if the camera envelope is expanded later

Expanding to court-height, partial-court or doubles footage needs an explicit supported-view specification and separate validation. The current review cannot silently change the initial envelope to make these clips count. Owner-supplied supported recordings and independent speed/3D references are currently unavailable.

- **Recover the acquisition clock:** obtain the publisher's original recording/export logs and an explicit mapping from timestamp-array entries to encoded decoded frames, including dropped frames, offsets and unit conventions. Compare the mapping with retained packet clocks and independently decoded presentation frames; examine gaps and beginning/middle/end synchronization. Cardinality mismatch makes direct array indexing invalid. A newly imposed CFR clock does not repair a missing acquisition mapping.
- **Recover calibration pixels and full-court ground truth:** obtain the publisher's actual court-annotation JSONs and documented image transform (including any eight-row padding/crop), verify calibrated metric coordinate axes, units and distortion, and independently annotate visible court landmarks and active-player foot contact points. Use synchronized alternative cameras to recover occluded points only after validating cross-camera timing and triangulation geometry. Preserve full uncertainty and unsupported visibility intervals. Do not infer clipped court corners from the selected model and then score that same model against them.
- **Obtain independent motion references:** synchronize independent surveyed position/timing measurements for players and independent ball speed/flight measurements such as appropriately validated radar, photogates or calibrated high-speed multi-view reconstruction. Independently annotate 2D joints/ball/racket visibility in synchronized views and adjudicate 3D reconstruction; report clock, calibration and labeling uncertainty. Agreement between predictions from related cameras is a consistency check, not automatically independent 3D or speed ground truth.
- **Freeze an eligible final set:** admit at least twelve supported clips across six actual independently reviewed sessions, with complete exclusions and independently adjudicated labels, before freezing the production bundle/metrics and running inference once. The current acquired directory and empty twelve-slot candidate plan remain acquisition history until those requirements are proven.

## Admission, trim, and freeze protocol

1. **Acquire only exact permitted assets when authorized.** Save the license/card and source revision, fetch one selected video with its actual matching timestamps/calibration, verify source byte count and SHA256/Git blob identity, and retain originals. Do not silently substitute a low-resolution publisher preview. CalTennis originals stay outside the public product assets. The JSON is a candidate list; null trim/label fields are intentionally unfilled evidence.
2. **Screen without model predictions.** A reviewer records camera orientation, singles roles, visible lines, actual date/session/athletes, unsupported intervals, resolution/codec, full presentation timestamps, and duplicate/event matches against development media. Replace a rejected candidate from another unused actual date. Do not choose windows because the selected model performs well on them.
3. **Select two temporally separate rallies per admitted session.** Target 10–20 seconds each with visible play plus reviewed absent/occluded intervals; keep source windows disjoint, separated by at least one completed rally or 30 seconds when the recording permits, and reserve all neighboring context/synchronized views in the same split. Different cameras of one event do not supply independent clips. Preserve full scene context rather than cropping out difficult players or spectators.
4. **Record exact time semantics before normalization.** Store source stream/time base, decoded presentation-order frame index, integer PTS/best-effort PTS, source start offset, decode failures, trim start and exclusive end PTS, and corresponding source frame indices. Verify timestamp-array length/units/monotonicity and its alignment to decoded frames. Filenames and nominal 60 Hz are not exact acquisition timestamps. Calibration coordinates/units/distortion and image scaling require independent verification.
5. **Keep a normalization map.** Every normalized frame needs its normalized PTS, selected original frame index/PTS, duplicate/drop disposition, and any resize/crop transform. Record FFmpeg version/filter/rounding policy and hashes of mapping/derived clip. Compare matched decoded frames at beginning, middle, end, cuts, and duplicate/drop intervals. CFR output duration alone cannot prove original timing or measurement scale. Source timing work is owned by the product processing implementation; this protocol defines the data it must retain.
6. **Freeze labels and grouping before inference.** Record at least twelve admitted clips / six actual sessions; video and label hashes; independent visibility/absence labels for ball, active players/identity, pose, racket/ownership, court landmarks, masks where applicable; reviewer adjudication; source grouping and cross-split exclusions; metrics/tolerances/acceptance thresholds; and production bundle hash. Save a separately named immutable final manifest. Keep this candidate manifest as acquisition history.
7. **Run the frozen qualification once.** Report per-clip and session-group results and uncertainty; analyze hard intervals and unsupported-view rejection. If model thresholds/selection are changed after seeing outcomes, reclassify those groups as development and acquire new final groups. Publisher calibration and multi-view agreement alone do not establish independent player/ball speed or 3D ground truth; those measurement gates still need independent references.

The next useful actions are replacement-source verification and eligibility review, then exact clock/calibration reconciliation and independent annotation of admitted views. This work has acquired a permitted noncommercial candidate pool and found concrete source/timing/view failures; it has not established six supported untouched sessions or twelve qualified clips.
