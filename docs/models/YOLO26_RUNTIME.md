# Selected YOLO26 runtime and reproducibility

Checked 8 October 2026. This candidate has **no independent tennis accuracy qualification** and lacks qualified production ball/court artifacts. One-epoch pilot weights remain isolated in `outputs`, with no promotion to configured production paths. Historical inference configurations are retained. The website must not silently substitute historical weights when this candidate is selected.

The three roles are YOLO26m Detect for ball, YOLO26s Pose for participants and 17 body keypoints, and YOLO26s Pose with a separate 14-landmark court head. The player pose model supplies the boxes as well as ankles. BoT-SORT settings are fixed in `configs/production/botsort_no_reid.yaml` with `with_reid: false`. Crop/tile passes reuse the same checkpoint.

The selected configuration is `configs/production/yolo26.yaml`, with artifact paths, roles and SHA256 values in `configs/production/yolo26.artifacts.json`. The bundle validates three roles, pinned runtime, shared participant pose, no learned ensemble/verifier/racket/ReID model, distinct court/human schemas, and matching weight hashes. A missing specialist checkpoint fails before inference. Artifact provenance must be present for ball/court promotion; a configuration is not evidence of training or accuracy.

Production promotion additionally requires a hashed external independent qualification receipt under [the qualification contract](../evaluation/YOLO26_QUALIFICATION.md). Boolean accuracy flags cannot promote a checkpoint. The receipt binds exact weights and operating/tracker settings to frozen human-reviewed independent matches, declared denominators/unknowns, retained references and numerical gates. The current candidate has no receipt and remains unqualified.

An explicit [private integrated evaluation entrypoint](../evaluation/YOLO26_PRIVATE_PIPELINE_EVALUATION.md) runs hash-pinned unqualified development pilots before independent qualification. It is bounded to 30 native frames, retains source PTS and resource/inference/decode receipts, and withholds measurement/event/scoring authority. The worker and default bundle keep the production qualification gate.

The local environment is Python 3.13, PyTorch 2.13.0+cu126, torchvision 0.28.0+cu126 and Ultralytics 8.4.36, already pinned in `requirements-product.txt`. It was retained instead of replacing the working environment. The RTX 4050 Laptop GPU has approximately 6 GB total VRAM; available memory varies with other processes. Training batch size must be measured rather than inferred from capacity alone.

Current official documentation uses `nms=False` to select YOLO26's one-to-one head, and newer export docs use `quantize`. The installed 8.4.36 source additionally controls prediction via `end2end`, and does not expose `quantize`. These adapters explicitly pass `end2end=True, nms=False` in that pinned runtime. No exported model is promoted. Export API changes must be tested against this installed version and conversion equivalence before changing production artifacts. Sources: [YOLO26](https://docs.ultralytics.com/models/yolo26/), [pose](https://docs.ultralytics.com/tasks/pose/), [end-to-end head](https://docs.ultralytics.com/guides/end2end-detection/), [export](https://docs.ultralytics.com/modes/export/).

Official initial weights were acquired from the publisher's `ultralytics/assets` release `v8.4.0`, into the isolated `models/yolo26` folder. `models/yolo26/provenance.json` records their URLs, bytes and hashes. The COCO detector checkpoint is **not** a tennis-trained specialist. The official human pose checkpoint is **not** a trained court detector.

Executed architecture smoke: `python -m scripts.setup.smoke_yolo26_models --device cpu`. `outputs/yolo26_model_smoke/evidence.json` records the actual run. On a synthetic 1920×1080 black frame, official participant pose at 960 and official detector at 1280 decoded original-coordinate Results; participant BoT-SORT initialization/reset ran. A custom one-class court pose head with `[14,3]` ran at 1280 after **795 compatible state-dict tensors were transferred and 84 tensors were initialized**. This verifies architecture/decoding, with no tennis detection or measurement accuracy claim and no trained court checkpoint. Cold load plus inference times from this run were approximately 0.459 s for participant pose, 1.077 s for initial detector and 0.922 s for the adapted court architecture. They are single-frame CPU checks, not throughput or live-latency evidence.

The ball adapter uses Ultralytics' restored tile coordinates, then adds the native crop origin once and clips to source dimensions. Overlapping candidates are deduplicated by configured box IoU or center distance. Player boxes retain local track IDs separately from anatomical evidence; a confident two-ankle midpoint remains a ground-contact estimate because jumping/occlusion/contact have not been independently qualified. Missing ankle evidence retains a declared bbox-bottom estimate. Court landmarks below confidence are masked unknown and retain fixed index semantics; calibration must use the remaining finite points or abstain.

Data preparation: `python -m scripts.data.prepare_yolo26_data --annotations <reviewed-native-labels.json> --output <new-dataset-version>`. This converts native-image labels, validates class/box/keypoint visibility, requires documented training permission and reviewed end-court singles framing, checks duplicates, and groups original match/session/source videos before deterministic 60/20/20 assignment. At least five independent groups are required to freeze those ratios. Existing development examples cannot become the final test.

Training preflight: `python -m scripts.train.train_yolo26_tennis --role court --manifest <frozen-manifest.json> --output <new-run>`. Add `--weights models/yolo26/yolo26s-pose.pt --execute` after the authorized frozen-data and memory checks pass. Ball uses `models/yolo26/yolo26m.pt`. Players are fine-tuned only with a recorded `--player-finetune-reason` justified by evaluation.

A small authorized learning check can run with `--pilot --smoke --epochs 1 --execute`. It excludes final-test records. If it uses its own training labels for loss/shape validation, the metadata explicitly marks that as an overfit check, not independent accuracy or checkpoint selection. Full controlled training requires reviewed independent final-test labels to be reserved, uses at most 150 epochs, batch measured on actual hardware, early stopping/resume/checkpoint hashes, and public arguments accepted by the pinned version. Reflections and mosaic are disabled; the court flip index is nevertheless recorded correctly. The training metadata lists compatible transferred and newly initialized tensors. No research-recipe-only flags are passed.

Executed private academic pilots use the publisher's [RacketVision dataset MIT declaration and training instructions](https://huggingface.co/datasets/linfeng302/RacketVision). Six native 1920×1080 frames from two elevated singles clips supply six manually visually reviewed ball boxes and six court instances/84 visible court landmarks. Reviewer identity is recorded as Codex agent manual visual review; independent human review remains pending. Ball and custom court14 each completed one GPU epoch at 1280, batch 1, workers 0 and AMP false. Logged GPU peaks were 5.04 GiB for ball and 2.92 GiB for court; court torch peak reserved was 3,149,922,304 bytes. `data/yolo26/pilot_training_receipts.json` preserves checkpoint/data/script hashes, runtime, losses and measured resources. Both same-training-label validation runs produced zero P/R/mAP; they establish an executable training path, with no accuracy or useful specialist claim.

The source registry and acquisition review are `data/yolo26/source_manifest.json` and `data/yolo26/acquisition_sources.json`. Publisher license, original footage permission, supported view, annotation quality and source/session independence have distinct status fields. The two accepted clips are grouped development-only because original match/session mapping is unresolved. Production specialist qualification requires independent human labels, untouched final-test sources and measured acceptance gates. Raw broadcast redistribution is not granted by this pilot decision. Local noncommercial research and public product redistribution have separate license checks; NC/ND is not treated as an automatic ban on private research adaptation. The appropriate Ultralytics AGPL/Enterprise route must also be recorded before product distribution. [Publisher licensing](https://www.ultralytics.com/license).
